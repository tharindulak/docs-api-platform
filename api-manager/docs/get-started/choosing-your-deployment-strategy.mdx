Selecting the right deployment strategy for WSO2 API Manager involves making two key, independent decisions based on your specific needs:

1.  **[Deployment Platform](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx)**: *Where* will your system run?
2.  **[Deployment Pattern](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx)**: *How* will the components be architected?

This guide presents common scenarios to help you choose the best combination of platform and pattern.

---

## Key Factors to Consider

Before choosing, evaluate your requirements based on these factors:

| Factor | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Scalability** | How much API traffic do you expect? Will it grow over time? Do you need to handle sudden traffic spikes? |
| **High Availability (HA)** | Can your business tolerate downtime? Do you need automatic failover and redundancy for all components? |
| **Security & Compliance**| What are your organization's security policies? Do you need to isolate traffic-facing components? |
| **Operational Overhead** | What is your team's expertise and preferred workflow (traditional infrastructure vs. cloud-native DevOps)? |

## Common Deployment Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Development and Testing
*   **If your primary need is...** simplicity and speed of setup for a non-production environment.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 0: Single Node](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-0-single-node)**
    *   **Platform**: Either **[Virtual Machines](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#on-premises-virtual-machines-vms)** for a traditional setup or a lightweight **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)** cluster.

### Scenario 2: Small-Scale Production with High Availability
*   **If your primary need is...** fault tolerance and reliability with minimal operational complexity.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 1: All-in-One HA](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-1-all-in-one-high-availability-ha)**
    *   **Platform**: Either **[Virtual Machines](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#on-premises-virtual-machines-vms)** or **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)**.

### Scenario 3: Scalable Gateway with a Simple Control Plane
*   **If your primary need is...** to scale only the API Gateway for traffic, while keeping the management components unified and simple.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 2: Simple Scalable Deployment](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-2-simple-scalable-deployment)**
    *   **Platform**: **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)** or **[Virtual Machines](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#on-premises-virtual-machines-vms)**.

### Scenario 4: Standard Production with High Traffic
*   **If your primary need is...** to independently scale multiple components (Gateway, Traffic Manager) to handle high API traffic with strong isolation.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 3: Recommended Distributed](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-3-recommended-distributed-deployment)**
    *   **Platform**: **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)** is ideal for its auto-scaling capabilities, but **[Virtual Machines](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#on-premises-virtual-machines-vms)** are also fully supported.

### Scenario 5: Ultimate Scalability & Isolation {#scenario-5-ultimate-scalability-isolation}
*   **If your primary need is...** maximum security, full component isolation, or integration with a central corporate IdP.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 4: Fully Distributed with Key Manager Seperation](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-4-fully-distributed-deployment-with-key-manager-separation)**
    *   **Platform**: **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)** or **[Virtual Machines](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#on-premises-virtual-machines-vms)**.

### Scenario 6: Targeted Scaling for Gateway & Identity {#scenario-6-targeted-scaling-for-gateway-identity}
*   **If your primary need is...** to scale the Gateway and also isolate the Key Manager for security or heavy authentication loads.
*   **Then the recommended approach is...**
    *   **Pattern**: **[Pattern 5: Simple Scalable with Key Manager Separation](./deployment-options/deployment-patterns.mdx#pattern-5-simple-scalable-with-key-manager-separation)**
    *   **Platform**: **[Kubernetes](./deployment-options/deployment-platforms.mdx#kubernetes-and-cloud-native)** or Virtual Machines.
